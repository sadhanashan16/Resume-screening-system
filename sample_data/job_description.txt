Job Title: Data Scientist

We are looking for a Data Scientist with strong experience in Python,
machine learning, and data analysis. The ideal candidate should have
hands-on experience with pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, and either
TensorFlow or PyTorch. Familiarity with SQL and cloud platforms
(AWS or Azure) is a plus. Candidates should have at least 2 years of
experience working on data science or machine learning projects, and
should hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science,
Engineering, or a related field. Strong communication skills and
experience working in Agile teams are desirable.
